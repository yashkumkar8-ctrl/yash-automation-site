Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Yash Automation handles information when a person messages the WhatsApp appointment assistant.

In short: the current demo processes the sender’s WhatsApp number and message text, including a requested appointment date and time, to provide the booking conversation. It does not provide medical advice.

Information processed

When a person uses the appointment assistant, it may process their WhatsApp phone number, messages they send, and the appointment date and time they request. Please do not send health records, symptoms, payment details, or other sensitive information to the demo.

Why it is used

Information is used to respond to the person’s request, guide the appointment conversation, and provide the requested confirmation. The service does not use these messages for advertising or sell personal information.

Storage and retention

In the current demo implementation, conversation state and booking entries are held in the running application’s memory and are cleared when that process stops or restarts. No booking database or long-term record system is currently configured. A clinic deployment may use different storage only if that is separately configured and disclosed.

Service providers

WhatsApp messages are delivered through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The demo server processes incoming webhook data to generate a reply.

Sharing

Information is not sold. It is shared with Meta as needed to deliver WhatsApp messages, and may be disclosed if required by law. Clinic staff should only receive booking details as agreed for the clinic’s workflow.

Your choices and requests

You can stop using the assistant at any time. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information associated with a conversation, contact Yash Automation at +91 76209 65180 and identify the WhatsApp conversation concerned. Because the current demo keeps booking entries only in memory, restarting the app clears those entries.

Security and changes

Reasonable safeguards are used for the demo, but no online service can promise absolute security. The demo should not be used to send medical or other sensitive records. This policy will be updated if the information collected, storage, or service providers change.
